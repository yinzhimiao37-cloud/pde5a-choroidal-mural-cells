## Exploratory ligand–receptor analysis

Ligand–receptor expression support was evaluated in GSE183320 using LIANA v1.10.0 and its consensus human resource. Author-provided log-normalized RNA expression and original donor and cell-type annotations were retained. Smooth muscle cells and pericytes were analysed separately as sender populations; endothelial cells and macrophages from the same donor served as receivers. Within each donor and mural-cell type, PDE5A-detected cells were divided at the median non-zero expression value into a higher-expression group (>median) and a lower-expression group (0<expression≤median). Ties were assigned to the lower-expression group. Comparisons with PDE5A-undetected cells were specified separately as sensitivity analyses. Each sender group required at least 10 cells, and each receiver population required at least 20 cells. PDE5A was excluded from the interaction resource and served only as a grouping variable.

LIANA geometric-mean scores were calculated for interactions with at least 10% expression support for every ligand and receptor subunit. Complexes were represented by their least-expressed subunit. Unsupported or unmeasured interactions were retained as unavailable and were not assigned a zero score. Higher- and lower-expression sender scores were paired within donors, with at least five eligible donors required per contrast. Mean paired differences, sample standard deviations, percentile intervals from 9,999 donor bootstrap samples and two-sided donor sign-flip P values were reported. Sign flips were enumerated for up to 15 donors; larger sets used 99,999 random sign assignments with a plus-one correction. Benjamini–Hochberg adjustment covered all evaluable cell-type, receiver and ligand–receptor contrasts within each prespecified analysis family. Positive candidate support required an adjusted P value below 0.05, a positive mean difference and concordant positive differences in at least 70% of donors.

Sensitivity analyses included control-labelled donors, exclusion of documented donors shared with GSE135922, leave-one-donor-out summaries and fixed-depth matching. For the latter, sender cells were stratified into quartiles of the pooled number of detected genes within each donor and mural-cell type, and equal numbers were sampled without replacement from the two groups within each stratum using a fixed seed. Residual differences in transcript counts and detected-gene counts were retained as technical diagnostics. This analysis was a post hoc extension of the initial localization analysis; its rules were fixed before new interaction results were calculated.

## Interpretation boundary for the Discussion

The interaction analysis describes transcript-supported cellular context. The two sender groups shared the same receiver population within each donor, so differences in interaction scores primarily reflect sender ligand expression and do not establish a receiver response. PDE5A transcript abundance does not measure enzyme activity or reproduce pharmacological inhibition. Detection-based groupings also depend on RNA capture and normalization, requiring particular caution when comparisons with undetected cells appear stronger than comparisons within detected cells. Candidate interactions should therefore be treated as hypotheses about mural-cell context, with the absence of a supported contrast interpreted in light of donor number and statistical precision.

## Implementation audit note for the Supplementary Information

An independent audit recomputed ligand–receptor scores directly from the input expression matrix. In the installed LIANA v1.10.0 implementation, the `return_all_lrs=True` branch removed duplicate complex rows before minimum-subunit aggregation, producing discrepancies for some multi-subunit interactions. The final pipeline used `return_all_lrs=False` to retain correct complex assembly, then joined the supported results to the complete resource to mark unsupported pairs as unavailable. Initial outputs were replaced, and the final scores were audited against direct calculations. This implementation correction did not alter the predefined cell groups, thresholds, resource, contrasts or inference rules.
